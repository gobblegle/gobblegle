Grid
Size: 5 rows × 5 columns
Use the + buttons around the grid to insert a row or column at that spot, and × to delete one (up to 10 × 10). Leave cells blank to create gaps. Type - in a cell to block it: it's saved as a gap, and New Word, Mixup and Fill Random never put a letter there. All letter cells must be connected (including diagonals).

Saving to:
1 · Setup
Regular boards start at 4×4 and Mini boards at 3×3. Your work here is kept in this browser until you push it, so a reload won't lose it.
2 · Big words
Green means the big word fits on an empty grid that size. ↻ picks a different word, ← goes back to the one before, or type your own.
3 · Boards
Type letters, - to block a tile, or a space to clear one. Use + and × around a grid to add or remove rows and columns. Tap a board's big word to type a different one (red = not in the dictionary). Tick OK on each board you're happy with. Only OK boards get pushed.
4 · Word review
Every word on every board above, in one list. Works just like Select Words in This Puzzle: ✓ approved, ✕ excluded from the valid list. Board word counts update as you go.
